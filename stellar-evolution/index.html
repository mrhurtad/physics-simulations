Evolutionary tracks are simplified, schematic models built for teaching. Values are approximate and are based on standard stellar-evolution results for solar metallicity; they are not the output of a stellar-structure code. Ages are in millions of years (Myr). Star sizes in the comparison panel use a compressed scale so that giants and white dwarfs fit on screen; the radius readout gives the true value. Inspired by Star in a Box.
