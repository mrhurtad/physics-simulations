Batteries are ideal except for a 1 mΩ internal resistance, and wires have 0.1 mΩ, so a short circuit shows up as an enormous current instead of breaking the solver. Circuits are solved with nodal analysis; capacitors are stepped in time every 2 ms. Concepts from OpenStax College Physics 2e, Ch. 21.
